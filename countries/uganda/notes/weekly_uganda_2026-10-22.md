# Uganda building costs — week of 22 October 2026

## In one line

Building material prices in Uganda are likely to rise — about 2.6% by January 2027.

## What it means for your money

For every **1,000,000 UGX** you spend on materials today, budget about **1,026,000 UGX** for the same materials in January 2027 (most likely between 1,008,000 and 1,064,000).

## Prices to watch

- ⬆ **Diesel** — up about 11% by January 2027
- ⬆ **All building inputs** — up about 2.6% by January 2027
- ⬆ **Rebar** — up about 2.5% by January 2027
- ⬆ **Cement** — up about 2.1% by January 2027
- ⬆ **Sand** — up about 1.2% by January 2027

## Why

- World oil prices are up 36% in the last 3 months — this feeds diesel, transport, asphalt and plastics.
- The UGX is 13% weaker against the US dollar than 3 months ago — imported steel, cement inputs, paint and fittings cost more.

## What to do this week

- Lock in prices for big orders now if you can.
- Add a price-adjustment clause to new contracts, or a bigger contingency.
- Ask suppliers how long their quotes are valid.
