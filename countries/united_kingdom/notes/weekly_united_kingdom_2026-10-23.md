# the United Kingdom building costs — week of 23 October 2026

## In one line

Building material prices in the United Kingdom are a little more likely than not to rise — about 1.2% by January 2027.

## What it means for your money

For every **1,000,000 GBP** you spend on materials today, budget about **1,012,000 GBP** for the same materials in January 2027 (most likely between 978,000 and 1,052,000).

## Prices to watch

- ⬆ **Materials for new housing** — up about 1.3% by January 2027
- ⬆ **Construction materials (all work)** — up about 1.2% by January 2027
- ⬆ **Materials for repair and maintenance** — up about 1.1% by January 2027

## Why

- World oil prices are up 15% in the last 3 months — this feeds diesel, transport, asphalt and plastics.
- The GBP has been steady against the US dollar.

## What to do this week

- Prices are creeping up: compare at least two quotes before big orders.
- Keep a small contingency for price rises in new quotes.
