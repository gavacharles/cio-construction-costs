Costs by material

Zimbabwe does not yet publish construction prices by material in a form we can use, but the latest official reading is ZimStat weighted Building Materials Price Index (BMPI), 2025-12: +7.8% on a year. Prices reported by participants (below) fill the gap, material by material.

What is driving costs

Oil

Brent crude: 115 USD a barrel (+35.5% in 13 weeks). Oil feeds diesel, transport, bitumen and plastics.

Currency

ZWG per US dollar: 26.6 (stronger 0.0% in 13 weeks) A weaker currency makes imported steel, cement inputs, fittings and paint dearer.
