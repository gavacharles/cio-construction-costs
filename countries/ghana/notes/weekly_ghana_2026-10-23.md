# Ghana building costs — week of 23 October 2026

## In one line

Import costs are pushing building prices in Ghana up — about 2.5% over the next 3 months (a little more likely than not).

## What it means for your money

For every **1,000,000 GHS** you spend on materials today, budget about **1,025,000 GHS** for the same materials in January 2027 (most likely between 948,000 and 1,078,000).

## Prices to watch

- ⬆ **Imported materials (steel, cement inputs, paint, pipes, fuel)** — up about 2.5% by January 2027
- ➡ **Local materials and labour** — not covered by this estimate; check local quotes.

## Why

- World oil prices are up 15% in the last 3 months — this feeds diesel, transport, asphalt and plastics.
- The GHS has been steady against the US dollar.

## What to do this week

- Lock in prices for big orders now if you can.
- Add a price-adjustment clause to new contracts, or a bigger contingency.
- Ask suppliers how long their quotes are valid.

## How CiO builds the estimate for Ghana

Ghana has no official construction price index, so **CiO Costs builds an estimate** every few hours:

- **What goes in:** world oil prices in the local currency and the exchange rate against the US dollar, weighted like the official construction baskets of Uganda and South Africa.
- **What it covers:** the imported part of building costs — steel, cement inputs, paint, pipes, fuel and transport.
- **What it misses:** local materials (sand, stone, blocks, timber) and labour. Your price reports fill this gap.

**The CiO estimate index** (100 = two years ago): May 86.3 · Jun 84.1 · Jul 84.6 · Aug 84.3 · Sep 87.1 · Oct 88.7.

**How reliable is it?** Where we can check it against an official index, its 3-month direction matched the official figure about 6 times in 10 (Uganda 63%, South Africa 64%). Use it as an early signal and a contract reference agreed by both sides, not as a measured price.

Using it in a contract: the monthly series is on the CiO Costs contract index page.

## How to read this

"Likely" means about 7 times in 10; "almost certain" about 9 in 10. Changes are measured from today. CiO Costs is a forecasting tool: these are estimates with a range, not official statistics, a price list or advice.
