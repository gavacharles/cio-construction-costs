Costs by material

Mauritius does not yet publish construction prices by material in a form we can use, but the latest official reading is Statistics Mauritius Construction Price Index (single-family house), 2026-03: +4.5% on a year. Prices reported by participants (below) fill the gap, material by material.

What is driving costs

Oil

Brent crude: 115 USD a barrel (+35.5% in 13 weeks). Oil feeds diesel, transport, bitumen and plastics.

Currency

MUR per US dollar: 47.3 (weaker 0.6% in 13 weeks) A weaker currency makes imported steel, cement inputs, fittings and paint dearer.
