## In one line

Building material prices in Austria are a little more likely than not to rise — about 0.7% by January 2027.

For every **1,000,000 EUR** you spend on materials today, budget about **1,007,000 EUR** for the same materials in January 2027 (most likely between 992,000 and 1,034,000).

- Keep a small contingency for price rises in new quotes.
