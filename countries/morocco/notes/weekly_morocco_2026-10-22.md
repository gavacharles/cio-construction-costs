# Morocco building costs — week of 22 October 2026

## In one line

Import costs for building in Morocco look steady for the next 3 months.

## What it means for your money

For every **1,000,000 MAD** you spend on materials today, budget about **1,000,000 MAD** for the same materials in January 2027 (most likely between 970,000 and 1,049,000).

## Prices to watch

- ➡ **Imported materials (steel, cement inputs, paint, pipes, fuel)** — about the same by January 2027
- ➡ **Local materials and labour** — not covered by this estimate; check local quotes.

## Why

- World oil prices are up 36% in the last 3 months — this feeds diesel, transport, asphalt and plastics.
- The MAD is 5% weaker against the US dollar than 3 months ago — imported steel, cement inputs, paint and fittings cost more.

## What to do this week
