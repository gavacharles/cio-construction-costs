# Ethiopia building costs — week of 22 October 2026

## In one line

Import costs are pushing building prices in Ethiopia up — about 1.8% over the next 3 months (likely).

## What it means for your money

For every **1,000,000 ETB** you spend on materials today, budget about **1,018,000 ETB** for the same materials in January 2027 (most likely between 995,000 and 1,051,000).

## Prices to watch

- ⬆ **Imported materials (steel, cement inputs, paint, pipes, fuel)** — up about 1.8% by January 2027
- ➡ **Local materials and labour** — not covered by this estimate; check local quotes.

## Why

- World oil prices are up 36% in the last 3 months — this feeds diesel, transport, asphalt and plastics.
- The ETB has been steady against the US dollar.

## What to do this week

- Prices are creeping up: compare at least two quotes before big orders.
- Keep a small contingency for price rises in new quotes.

## Official figures

Ethiopia has no official construction price index yet. This is an estimate from oil and currency moves.

## How to read this

"Likely" means about 7 times in 10; "almost certain" about 9 in 10. Changes are measured from today. These are estimates, not advice.
