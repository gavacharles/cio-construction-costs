Privacy

Privacy policy

What CiO Costs collects, why, and what it never does. There are no accounts, no advertising and no tracking cookies.

1. What we collect

  • Price reports: country, item, price, town or area, whether it is the main city or elsewhere, where you bought, when you paid, and — only if you choose to give it — a phone number or email for follow-up questions. Each reporter is identified only by a scrambled code derived from the connection, so the same person counts once; the connection address itself is not stored with the report.
  • Price alerts: your email and what you asked to be alerted about, with your consent.
  • Pro waitlist: your email, and optionally your organisation and role, with your consent.
  • Visit counts: the page and actions (for example "a price was reported"), language, and the website you came from. A visitor is a code that changes every day, so you cannot be followed from one day to the next. No cookies and no IP addresses are stored. Browsers that send "Do Not Track" are not counted.
  • On your own device only: your country, language, bills of quantities and IPC entries are kept in your browser (local storage). They are not sent to us.

2. How it is used

To publish typical prices, indices and forecasts (only as combinations of at least three people's reports), to send the alerts and emails you asked for, to check data quality, and to understand which parts of the site are useful.

3. What we never do

We never publish single reports, names, numbers or emails; never sell or rent personal data; never use it for advertising.

4. Who can see it

Only the CiO Lab team running the site. Data is kept on the site's own server; service providers used to run it (hosting, email sending) process it only on our instructions.

5. How long

Price reports are kept to build history (the published figures are aggregates). Contact details given with a report, alert sign-ups and waitlist entries are deleted on request, and alert and waitlist entries when the service ends.

6. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete personal data you gave us, or withdraw consent for emails, at any time (every email has a one-click way to stop). We aim to follow the data protection laws of the countries we serve, including Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019, Kenya's Data Protection Act 2019, South Africa's POPIA and Nigeria's Data Protection Act 2023.
