Egypt does not yet publish construction prices by material in a form we can use. Prices reported by participants (below) fill the gap, material by material.

What is driving costs

Oil

Brent crude: 115 USD a barrel (+35.5% in 13 weeks). Oil feeds diesel, transport, bitumen and plastics.

Currency

EGP per US dollar: 53.4 (weaker 5.9% in 13 weeks) A weaker currency makes imported steel, cement inputs, fittings and paint dearer.
