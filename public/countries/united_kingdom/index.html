How this test works

  1. Every month in the past four years, we made a forecast using only the figures that were published by then.
  2. We compared it with the official figure that came out later: did it land inside our likely range?
  3. We also compare with the simplest rule — “prices stay where they are” — to see whether the forecast adds anything.
