What is driving costs

Oil

Brent crude: 115 USD a barrel (+35.5% in 13 weeks). Oil feeds diesel, transport, bitumen and plastics.

Currency

ZMW per US dollar: 20.1 (weaker 9.9% in 13 weeks) A weaker currency makes imported steel, cement inputs, fittings and paint dearer.
