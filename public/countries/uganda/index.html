What is driving costs

Oil

Brent crude: 115 USD a barrel (+14.9% in 13 weeks). Oil feeds diesel, transport, bitumen and plastics.

Currency

UGX per US dollar: 4,294.4 (weaker 15.2% in 13 weeks) A weaker currency makes imported steel, cement inputs, fittings and paint dearer.
