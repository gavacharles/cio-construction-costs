Adjust a payment

Formula: adjusted amount = amount × (a + b × I₁ / I₀), where a is the fixed part, b = 1 − a, I₀ the index in the base month and I₁ in the valuation month — the one-index form of standard price adjustment clauses.

How to use it — and its limits

  • Official months come straight from the statistics office (UBOS, KNBS, Stats SA). Provisional months are our forecast, used only until the official figure is published; agree in the contract that provisional values are replaced.
  • Countries without an official construction index (Nigeria, Rwanda, Tanzania, Zambia) show our import-cost index from oil and the exchange rate. It does not cover local materials or labour, so use it with care and say so in the contract.
  • This is free during the pilot. Indices by material and city, long history, monthly certificates and a data feed are planned for CiO Costs Pro.
