How it works

Full detail in FORECASTING.md.

1 · Inputs

Official indices (UBOS, KNBS, Stats SA and others), Brent, 18 exchange rates, rainfall, public spending and sourced tax measures, refreshed weekly.

2 · Models

Uganda: structural model on UBOS's own weights. Kenya: quarterly driver model. South Africa: contract nowcast. Others: cost-pressure outlook.

3 · Checks

Every model is backtested month by month and kept only if it beats simple benchmarks; ranges are calibrated to cover 80% of past outcomes.

4 · Outputs

Weekly notes, the participant app's forecast page, and the opening prices of the forecasting contracts.
